1. Who we are

Jarv Channel Assistant ("the app") is a private, internal tool operated by Tibor Dívó (Perfect Visuals Studio, Hungary) to manage his own YouTube channels. It is not available to the public. Contact: studio.perfectvisuals@gmail.com.

2. YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API v3 and YouTube Analytics API). By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

3. What information the app accesses

Only for the channels the owner has explicitly authorized through Google OAuth: channel details and settings, videos and their metadata, playlists, comments on the owner's videos, and channel analytics (views, watch time, audience and traffic reports). The app does not access any other Google account data.

4. How the information is used

Solely to carry out actions the channel owner requests: uploading his videos, editing titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails, managing playlists and comments, and preparing statistics reports for the owner. The information is never used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to managing the owner's own channels.

5. How the information is shared

The information is not sold, rented or shared with third parties for their own purposes. The owner operates the app through his personal AI assistant, which runs on Anthropic's Claude service; the data needed for a requested action (for example a video title or a statistics table) is processed there only to perform that action for the owner. No information is shared internally beyond the owner himself.

6. Storage, retention and deletion

  • OAuth refresh tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own computer and are never published.
  • The app does not keep a database of YouTube API data. Data retrieved for a task is used for that task; any copy saved in a report is deleted or refreshed within 30 days.
  • To request deletion of any stored data, write to studio.perfectvisuals@gmail.com; it will be deleted within 7 days.

7. Cookies and device storage

The app is a command-line tool and does not place cookies or other tracking technologies on any user's device or browser. This website does not use cookies or analytics.

8. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After revocation the app can no longer access the channel, and the stored token is deleted.

9. Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and to the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.

10. Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
