Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Jarv YouTube is a private tool that accesses YouTube data only for the channels its owner has explicitly authorized through Google OAuth.

Data accessed

YouTube channel, video, playlist, comment and analytics data of the authorized channels, through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API.

How data is used

Only to perform actions requested by the channel owner (uploading and editing videos, managing playlists and comments, reporting statistics). Data is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Storage

OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own computer. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
