Jarv Channel Assistant

A private channel management tool by Tibor Dívó (Perfect Visuals Studio), Hungary

Jarv Channel Assistant is a private, internal tool that its owner, Tibor Dívó, uses to manage his own YouTube channels. It runs only on the owner's own computer and is operated by the owner through his personal AI assistant.

What it does

  • Uploads the owner's own videos to his own channels and sets titles, descriptions, tags, language and thumbnails.
  • Edits the metadata of existing videos and manages playlists.
  • Reads and replies to comments on the owner's own videos, with the owner's approval.
  • Reads channel analytics and produces reports for the owner.

It is not offered, sold or distributed to anyone else. Only the channel owner can authorize access to his channels.
